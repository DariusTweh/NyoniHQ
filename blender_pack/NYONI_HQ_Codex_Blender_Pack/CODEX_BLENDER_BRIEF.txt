1. ART DIRECTION
Create a premium miniature architectural 3D studio with the clean, open isometric character of Towers AI, but original for Nyoni Studios.
- Warm ivory and soft-white walls; pale natural oak flooring
- Floor-to-ceiling windows with slim charcoal frames and glass
- Warm directional sunlight; realistic contact shadows; nuanced indirect fill
- Cream/stone fabrics, natural woods, restrained metal, subtle glass
- Beveled, tactile geometry; polished, slightly stylized proportions
- Spatial breathing room and obvious walking paths
- Cute small white bots with expressive black display faces and restrained department accent colors
- Minimal signage and no distracting UI rendered onto walls
Avoid a dark cyberpunk aesthetic, saturated glowing outlines, overdecorated desks, disproportionately oversized mascots, or a flat CSS-style illustration. Make the actual 3D room the centerpiece.

2. FIRST ENVIRONMENT: NYONI COMMERCIALS
Target floor footprint approximately 16m wide x 12m deep, ceiling around 3.4m. These dimensions are a starting guideline; tune room dimensions to match the images and improve staging. Build a cutaway office with two major perimeter walls and partial front edges so all work areas remain visible from an elevated orthographic three-quarter view.
